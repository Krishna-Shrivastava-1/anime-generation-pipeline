1. Every small action, reaction, gesture, or movement MUST be its own distinct "beat" inside a scene. Never put multiple visual actions into one beat.
2. In `assets`, specify detailed "visual_dna" for every character, environment, and critical recurring prop. Describe faces, hair, clothing, lighting, and textures explicitly.
3. Every `prompt_action` MUST use the exact placeholder syntax: `{assets.<asset_key>}`.

5. For "in_place_action", the `session_delta_prompt` should instruct the image generator to modify the previous image ("In the same scene, keep character and room identical, change action to...").
6. The `narration_text` must only contain the exact sentence snippet spoken during that specific beat (usually 2 to 6 seconds worth of speech).
7. Return ONLY valid, raw JSON conforming exactly to the template below.
