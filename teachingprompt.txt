============================================================

- STRICT CONCATENATION RULE: Joining every clip's `spoken_text` inside a beat (with spaces) MUST reproduce the beat's `narration` word-for-word, including punctuation.
- `beat_id` must be sequential (`B0001`, `B0002`, ...).
- `clip_id` must be GLOBALLY UNIQUE and sequential across the entire JSON (`C0001`, `C0002`, `C0003`, ...), because each clip maps to `build/frames/{clip_id}.png`.
